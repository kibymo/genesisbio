          How to prepare the antigen that is in SDS-PAGE before delivering                  to 
          Genesis Biotech Inc?</b>         
                 
          </TD>     
        </TR>
        <TR>
          <TD valign="top" width="507" height="57">
          <font size="2">If the client provide the antigen in SDS-PAGE, we suggest them prepare the antigen following the pre-process below. Or animals will die from being injected with large concentration of acrylamide gel antigen.　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　<br>●Concentrate the sample as possible as you can. 
          　　　　　　　　　　　　　<br>●Load the sample in one piece of acrylamide gel if possible.　　　　　　　　　　　<br>●Run mingled or std. Gels (1 mm or 1.5 mm thickness). Use a big flat comb with no                        
          wells.　 　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　<br>●Stain the gel with coomassie blue and then destain it. (Decrease the toxicity of acrylamid gel.)　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　<br>●Cut the clear and firm band out of the gel. Carefully cut along the edge of the                            
          band.</font>
