          <b class="primaryHeader">
          Why do we need to conjugate KLH or BSA to Peptide?</b>         
                 
          </TD>     
        </TR>
        <TR>
          <TD valign="top" width="507" height="79">
          <font size="2">Because the molecular weight of peptide is too small, which is unable to stimulate B cell to produce antibody. Therefore, they need to rely on a carrier protein to perform immunogen ability. KLH and BSA both are often used as carrier proteins, however KLH is always a better choice to enhance animal immuno response. ( KLH : Keyhole Limpet Hemocyanin ; MWt : 3,000,000~7,500,000.).</font>
